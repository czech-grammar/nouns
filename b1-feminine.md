# B1 Czech: feminine nouns

Draft word list for the picture-card deck, B1 level. Grouped by topic (the deck's
topic keys), not by corpus frequency.

Every word comes from `tools/b1/candidates-f-4000.json`: the 404 feminine nouns in
the SYN2015 frequency list (ranks 1–4000) that the deck does not have yet. Sorted
into three groups: the **picturable core** (a concrete object, person, animal,
place, food or scene a child could recognise in a small cartoon – the target for
drawing), the **abstract** words (useful B1 vocabulary, kept for a possible later
sentence-card tier), and the words **left out on purpose**.

Patterns: **žena** (hard, -a), **růže** (soft, -e/-ě, -ice), **píseň** (soft
consonant, gen. -ě/-e), **kost** (consonant, gen. -i); a note in parentheses
gives an irregular form. Rank: position in the SYN2015 frequency list.

## Left out on purpose

Every candidate not kept above, grouped by reason, so nothing is silently lost.

- Not a noun (adverb mis-tagged in the source list): dále.
- Variants or duplicates of words already in the deck: lahev (spelling variant of láhev, already in the A2 list), prostora (rare variant of prostor), ženská (colloquial, slightly dismissive word for žena; adjective declension).
- Proper-noun-like: vysočina (in the corpus almost always the Vysočina region).
- Violent, weapons and death: vražda, sebevražda, mrtvola, bitva, zbraň, pistole, střela.
- Adult: cigareta, panna (virgin; maiden – mostly heads-or-tails and the star sign).
- Medical-clinical: terapie, deprese.
- Technical and scientific: částice, molekula, kyselina, hornina, emise, interakce, izolace.
- Bureaucratic, economic, political and journalistic jargon: komise, dispozice, dotace, investice, akcie, finance, sazba, koalice, reforma, korupce, kampaň, redakce, nemovitost, poptávka, novela, problematika, asociace, charakteristika, koncepce.
- Nominalisations: realizace, produkce, instalace, publikace, integrace, výstavba.

Borderline calls: hrůza (horror) is kept in the abstract group because it is an
everyday exclamation ("to je hrůza!") rather than a violent word; oběť (victim,
sacrifice) is kept there for the same reason. zbraň and pistole are objects rather
than acts, but a children's picture deck does not need weapon cards, so they go
with vražda. cigareta is an object too, but an adult one. buňka, rovnice, křivka
and statistika stay in the abstract group as school words; částice, molekula,
kyselina and hornina are a step more technical and are left out.
