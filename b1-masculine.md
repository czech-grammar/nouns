# B1 Czech: masculine nouns

Draft word list for the B1 picture-card deck, same format as `a2-masculine.md`.
Grouped by topic, not by frequency. Masculine splits into **animate (Ma, dark
blue)** – people and animals – and **inanimate (Mi, light blue)** – everything
else.

Every word comes from `tools/b1/candidates-m-4000.json`: the 467 masculine
nouns in ranks 1–4000 of the SYN2015 frequency list that the deck does not
have yet. Each word is sorted into exactly one of three groups: the
**picturable core** (a person, animal, thing, place, food or scene that fits in
one small cartoon), the **abstract** tier (useful B1 words that need a
sentence card rather than a picture) and **left out** (jargon, technical
terms, loans, duplicates). The Rank column is the SYN2015 rank.

Patterns. Animate: **pán** (hard), **muž** (soft), **předseda** (-a),
**soudce** (-ce). Inanimate: **hrad** (hard), **stroj** (soft). A note in
parentheses gives an irregular genitive or plural; "adjective declension"
marks nouns that decline like adjectives (vrchní, rozhodčí, vedoucí). Two
oddities: **hrabě** declines like kuře (hraběte) and is neuter in the plural
(hrabata); **rys** is animate only as the lynx – the far commoner "feature,
trait" sense is inanimate (rysu, pl. rysy).

No food or time words this round; both sections are empty and left out.

## Notes for the drawings

- The 15 people in Ma 1 need distinct props: author with a manuscript, boss
  at a desk, soldier in uniform, scientist with a flask, captain at a ship's
  wheel, architect with a blueprint, priest in a collar, general with medals,
  pilot with headset, photographer with camera, pope with mitre and staff,
  head waiter with a bow tie and tray, bishop with a crozier.
- The sport people (Ma 6) work as one football scene split into cards:
  hráč, útočník, brankář, obránce, rozhodčí with a whistle, soupeř as the
  other shirt colour, vítěz on a podium, mistr with a trophy belt, jezdec on
  a horse.
- Fairy-tale Ma 7: duch (sheet ghost), obr, král with crown, císař with a
  bigger crown, hrabě with a monocle, hrdina with a cape, anděl with wings,
  agent in sunglasses, zloděj with a striped shirt and sack, vězeň behind bars.
- Face-emotion cards in Mi body: úsměv, smích, smutek, vztek; plus sen
  (sleeper with a thought bubble), dech (breath on a cold day), pot.
- Tree cards in Mi other: kořen, kmen, květ, plod, next to A2 list (leaf).
- Fire cluster: oheň (campfire), plamen (single flame), kouř, výbuch, blesk.
- Words needing an agreed metaphor: cíl (target with arrow / finish tape),
  vzduch (balloon, wind), stín, vesmír (planets and stars), chod (three
  plates in a row), výhled (person at a viewpoint), roh (street corner and an
  animal horn in one picture), pruh (striped shirt beside a lane marking).
