# A1 completion: official A1 nouns not yet in the deck

Words to draw to finish A1. Every word here is rated **A1 in the Referenční
popis češtiny pro účely zkoušky z českého jazyka pro trvalý pobyt v ČR** (the
official residence-exam description, appendix "Soupis lexikálních jednotek";
parsed copy in `tools/official-nouns.json`). Chosen from the 415 official A1
nouns the deck lacked, keeping the ones a child can picture and leaving the
administrative and medical-bureaucratic vocabulary (nemocenská, neschopenka,
pojištěnec, kolek, apostila …) for a possible adult deck.

Words marked ✔ were already in our reserve lists. Patterns as in the other
lists; a note in parentheses gives an irregular form.

## Left out on purpose

- Administrative and medical-bureaucratic A1: advokát, adresát, doklad,
  důchod, kolek, nájemník, nemocenská, neschopenka, pojištěnec, pronájem,
  smlouva, průkaz, podpis, pobyt, poplatek, úřad-related forms.
- Nationalities (Rus, Ukrajinec, Vietnamec, Mongol and feminine forms).
- Abbreviations and loan items: CD, DVD, MP3, PIN, fax, empétrojka.
- Units: centimetr, kilogram, kilometr, litr, metr, gram, procento.
- Reserve words the official list rates A2 or does not list at all stay in
  the reserve sections of the three A1 lists; they can go into the A2 deck.
