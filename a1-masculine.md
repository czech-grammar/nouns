# A1 Czech: masculine nouns

Draft word list for the picture-card deck, same format as the feminine and
neuter lists. Masculine splits into **animate (Ma, dark blue)** – people and
animals – and **inanimate (Mi, light blue)** – everything else.

Patterns. Animate: **pán** (hard), **muž** (soft), **předseda** (-a),
**soudce** (-ce). Inanimate: **hrad** (hard), **stroj** (soft). A note in
parentheses gives an irregular genitive or plural.

## Notes for the drawings

- The 24 people must be visibly different men and boys: vary age, hair,
  clothes and a defining prop (chef's hat, police cap, waiter's tray, steering
  wheel, camera and map for the tourist, a Czech flag T-shirt for Čech).
- pes and kůň are animate (Ma, dark blue) even though they are animals.
- Months get one seasonal scene each, sharing a small calendar tab with the
  month number so they read as a set.
- Abstract words needing an agreed metaphor: čas, den, týden, rok, víkend,
  svět, sport, internet, úřad, nákup, účet, úkol, test, text, jazyk.
