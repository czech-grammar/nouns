# B1 Czech: neuter nouns

Draft word list for the B1 picture-card deck, same format as `a2-neuter.md`.
Grouped by topic, not by frequency. Colour on the cards: green.

Every word comes from `tools/b1/candidates-n-4000.json`: the 164 neuter nouns
in ranks 1–4000 of the SYN2015 frequency list that the deck does not have yet.
Each word is sorted into exactly one of three groups: the **picturable core**
(a thing, place, material, food, animal or scene that fits in one small
cartoon), the **abstract** tier (useful B1 words that need a sentence card
rather than a picture) and **left out** (jargon, technical terms, pure
nominalisations). The Rank column is the SYN2015 rank.

Patterns: **město** (hard, -o), **moře** (soft, -e), **kuře** (-e, gen. -ete),
**stavení** (-í, including every verbal noun in -ní/-tí). Loans in -um
(album, studium) decline like město with the -um dropped (alba, studia); the
Greek loans in -ma (téma, drama, schéma) add -at- (tématu, pl. témata);
finále does not decline at all.

No meals or shopping words this round; both sections are empty and left out.

## Notes for the drawings

- The three ages (dětství, mládí, stáří) work as a set: the same figure as a
  child, a young adult and an old person with a stick.
- The school set (čtení, psaní, učení, umění, písmo) can share one desk and
  one child; only the object on the desk changes.
- The sport set (utkání, vítězství, mužstvo, publikum) shares one pitch and
  one stand; each card zooms on a different part of it.
- The feelings (překvapení, tajemství, přátelství) need a strong single
  metaphor each, like štěstí and rozloučení in A2; worth agreeing before
  drawing.

## Left out on purpose – 37 words

- Bureaucratic, political and sector jargon: sdružení, zastupitelstvo,
  zdravotnictví, školství, financování, vyšetřování, zajištění.
- Technical and scientific: těleso, záření, kritérium, ložisko, rozhraní,
  spektrum.
- Pure nominalisations with no scene (the verb carries the meaning, or an
  abstract entry already covers it, e.g. rozhodování → rozhodnutí,
  porovnání → srovnání, používání → použití): zpracování, zvýšení, snížení,
