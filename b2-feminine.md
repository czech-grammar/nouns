# B2 Czech: feminine nouns

Draft word list for the picture-card deck, B2 level. Grouped by topic (the deck's
topic keys), not by corpus frequency.

Every word comes from `tools/b2/candidates-f-8000.json`: the 495 feminine nouns in
ranks 4,001–8,000 of the SYN2015 frequency list that the deck (A1, A2 and B1,
1,237 cards) does not have yet. Sorted into three groups: the **picturable core**
(a concrete object, person, animal, place, food or scene a child could recognise
in a small cartoon – the target for drawing, now with a one-line picture idea for
the artist), the **abstract** words (useful B2 vocabulary, kept for a possible
later sentence-card tier), and the words **left out on purpose**. B2 words are
odder than the A1–B1 core: more countryside, more workshop, more fairy tale.

Patterns: **žena** (hard, -a), **růže** (soft, -e/-ě, -ice, -ie), **píseň** (soft
consonant, gen. -ě/-e), **kost** (consonant, gen. -i); a note in parentheses
gives an irregular form. Rank: position in the SYN2015 frequency list.

## Notes for the drawings

- The 13 people in family are a fairy-tale and a workplace set: princezna,
  bohyně, čarodějnice, stráž, stařena and kráska go with the B1 král, obr and
  duch; autorka, kolegyně, modelka, doktorka and pacientka need distinct props.
  doktorka and pacientka work as one consulting-room scene split in two.
- Farm cluster across animals and travel: ovce, husa, stáj, stodola, pastvina,
  sláma, studna, ves, next to the B1 statek and A2 koza and kachna; keep the same
  fence and barn colours so the cards read as one place.
- Water cluster: hráz (low grassy bank), přehrada (tall concrete wall), propast,
  příď, plachta; keep hráz and přehrada clearly different in scale.
- Kitchen chemistry: pára, pěna, plíseň, špetka, slupka – small close-ups, no
  people needed except the hand.
- Instruments: kytara, trubka, varhany, struna, nota, klávesa join the A2 housle
  and piano; draw struna and klávesa as close-ups of the same guitar and piano.
- Two-sense cards (both senses drawn): zásuvka, klika, řasa, čočka, panenka,
  stolička, hůl, páska, pila, vločka, siréna, ploutev, trubka, figura/figurka.
- Body cards: čelist, páteř, kostra, hrst, křeč – use the same outline figure as
  the B1 lebka and hruď so the anatomy series is consistent.
- Space: galaxie, kometa next to the B1 planeta and A1 hvězda – same dark-blue
  background.
- Pattern words worth a hint on the card: ves (vsi), hůl (hole), nit (niti/nitě),
  pouť (pouti/poutě), moucha (mouše).

## Left out on purpose

Every candidate not kept above, grouped by reason, so nothing is silently lost.

- Not a noun (mis-tagged in the source list): van (in the corpus the genitive
  plural of vana, already in the A1 list; the masculine noun van "gust" is poetic).
- Variants, diminutives or duplicates of words already in the deck: skříňka
  (diminutive of skříň), zahrádka (of zahrada), hlavička (of hlava), cestička (of
  cesta), lahvička (of láhev), mamka (colloquial for máma), muzika (colloquial for
  hudba), půle (variant of půlka / polovina), universita (spelling variant of
  univerzita), lednice (same appliance as lednička), lázeň (singular of the B1
  lázně), ledvina (singular of the A2 ledviny), sanitka (same vehicle as
  záchranka), party (English loan for večírek / oslava; indeclinable).
- Violent, weapons and death: bomba, puška, kulka, střelba, spoušť (trigger),
  rakev, hrobka, poprava, invaze, okupace.
- Adult or vulgar, or not for a children's deck: prdel, milenka, prostitutka, podprsenka.
- Colloquial or dismissive: huba, sranda, blbost.
- Alcohol: whisky (indeclinable), vodka.
- Medical-clinical: infekce, diagnóza, mutace.
- Technical and scientific: tkáň, dioda, veličina, převodovka, komponenta,
  bílkovina, kapalina, živina, elektroda, membrána, sloučenina, zemina, sonda,
  deformace.
- Bureaucratic, economic, political and journalistic jargon: eurozóna, opozice,
  vyhláška, pravomoc, sankce, ústava, evidence, legislativa, demonstrace, hypotéka,
  inflace, burza, tržba, bilance, transakce, směrnice, působnost, inspekce,
  ideologie, mocnost, petice, federace, privatizace, intervence, metodika,
  implementace, aktualizace.
- Nominalisations: likvidace, distribuce, transformace, adaptace, redukce,
  konzumace, reprodukce, korekce.

Borderline calls: lžička, ručička, stolička, tyčinka, panenka and figurka are
diminutives in form but name different objects (teaspoon, clock hand, molar,
pretzel stick, doll, game piece), so they stay in the picturable core; skříňka,
zahrádka, hlavička, cestička and lahvička only mean "small X" and go out. police
is kept although the deck has polička – the deck has the diminutive, this is the
base word. kostra (skeleton) stays as anatomy next to the B1 lebka; rakev and
hrobka are death words and go out. kalhotky and podprsenka are ordinary clothing
like the A2 plavky and prádlo; podprsenka was dropped from the core as not for a children's deck.
bakterie, evoluce, gravitace, souřadnice, množina and geometrie stay in the
abstract group as school words; tkáň, bílkovina, sloučenina and živina are a step
more technical and are left out. pravice and levice are kept as body/politics
double words rather than left out with the political jargon. herna is kept
abstract because its two senses (nursery playroom, gaming hall) pull in opposite
directions. sedačka, toaleta, vyhlídka, vozovka, klenba, stráň and usedlost are
concrete but would duplicate an existing card (sedadlo, záchod, výhled, silnice,
oblouk, svah, statek), so they sit in the abstract group.
