# A1 Czech: feminine nouns

Draft word list for the picture-card deck. Grouped by topic (the way A1
courses and the CCE-A1 exam are organised), not by corpus frequency.

Patterns: **žena** (hard, -a), **růže** (soft, -e/-ě, -ice), **píseň** (soft
consonant, gen. -ě/-e), **kost** (consonant, gen. -i). ✔ = already in the pilot deck.
